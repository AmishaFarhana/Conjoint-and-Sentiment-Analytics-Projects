# 📊 Conjoint Analysis & Sentiment Analytics

This project applies marketing analytics techniques to two cases:

1️⃣ Forte Executive Innes – Conjoint Analysis  
2️⃣ Robinhood – Crisis Sentiment & Emotion Analysis  

The goal was to use data-driven methods to support strategic product and communication decisions.

---

# 1️⃣ Forte Executive Innes – Conjoint Analysis

## 📌 Business Problem

Forte, a European hotel brand, planned to launch a new mid-priced hotel concept in the U.S. targeting business travelers.

Objective:
Identify the optimal hotel concept that maximizes market share and revenue in the State College market.

---

## 🔎 Methodology

- Conducted conjoint analysis using preference data from 40 business travelers
- Compared four concepts:
  - Professional 1
  - Professional 2
  - Tourist
  - Deluxe
- Used first-choice decision rule to simulate market share
- Calculated weighted revenue (Market Share × Revenue per Unit)

---

## ✅ Key Results

The **Tourist concept** delivered:

- Highest weighted revenue: **$12.6 per customer**
- Tied highest predicted market share: **12.5%**
- Strong feature balance (large rooms, exercise + pool, business amenities)

Although Professional 1 achieved similar market share, the Tourist concept generated slightly higher profitability.

---

## 🎯 Recommendation

Launch the **Tourist concept** in State College.

It balances:
- Market appeal
- Revenue potential
- Cost efficiency

# 2️⃣ Robinhood – Crisis Sentiment & Emotion Analysis

## 📌 Business Problem

During the GameStop trading halt, Robinhood faced a reputational crisis.

Objective:
Analyze shifts in public sentiment and emotional tone before, during, and after the crisis.

---

## 🔎 Methods Used

- Word Cloud Analysis (with stemming)
- Valence (Sentiment) Analysis
- Emotion Analysis (Trust, Anger, Fear, Joy, etc.)

Time Periods:
- Before crisis
- During crisis
- After crisis

---

## 📊 Key Findings

### 🟢 Word Cloud Shifts

- **Before:** General trading terms (“stock”, “free”, “join”)
- **During:** Crisis-focused terms (“gamestop”, “restrict”, “suspend”)
- **After:** Shift toward crypto (“dogecoin”) and recovery discourse

---

### 📉 Sentiment (Valence) Trends

| Period   | Positive | Negative |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Before   | 58%      | 18%      |
| During   | 31%      | 50%      |
| After    | 43%      | 34%      |

During the crisis:
- Negative sentiment nearly doubled
- Positive sentiment sharply declined

After the crisis:
- Trust partially recovered
- Sentiment stabilized but did not fully return to baseline

---

### 😡 Emotion Analysis

**Before:**
- High trust (24%)
- Anticipation dominant

**During:**
- Fear and anger increased significantly
- Trust declined
- Heightened emotional volatility

**After:**
- Trust rebounded
- Anticipation increased
- Residual skepticism remained

---

## 🎯 Strategic Insights

### For Forte:
- Conjoint analysis enables revenue-optimized concept selection
- Feature trade-offs must be evaluated via simulated share, not intuition

### For Robinhood:
- Real-time sentiment monitoring is critical
- Crisis periods dramatically amplify negative emotions
- Communication strategy should address fear and anger immediately
- Post-crisis trust rebuilding is gradual and measurable
